What is Covered in the Prep Pack?

1. Licensed Electrician's Theory (LET)

This section focuses heavily on navigating the AS/NZS 3000 Standards. We walk you through how to calculate maximum demand using Table C1 and C2, selecting correct cables using AS/NZS 3008 tables based on installation methods, and checking voltage drop constraints to ensure installation safety. Our pack includes sample calculation templates that you can download and practice with.

2. Licensed Electrician's Practical (LEP)

The practical exam requires you to wire a meter panel and find 10 visual defects on a simulated board. We provide an interactive visual simulator detailing common defects (e.g., lack of protection, incorrect colors, poor connection tightness) and clear references to AS/NZS 3000 clauses so you can write down the exact reasons in your exam paper.

3. Safe Working Practice (SWP)

Safety is non-negotiable. One wrong testing sequence or skipping a lock-tag step results in an instant fail. We layout the exact 12-step sequence for safe isolation testing, testing RCDs, checking for zero voltage, and verifying earthing systems to ensure you build bulletproof muscle memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

What references am I allowed to use in the LET exam?
The Victoria LET assessment is an open-book exam. You are permitted to bring physical copies of AS/NZS 3000 (Wiring Rules), AS/NZS 3008.1.1 (Cable Selection), AS/NZS 3012 (Construction Sites), and the Victorian Electrical Safety Regulations. Note that no loose notes are allowed inside the books.

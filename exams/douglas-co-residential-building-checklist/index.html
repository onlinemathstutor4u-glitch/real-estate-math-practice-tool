Colorado Construction Standards

Douglas County CO Residential Building Checklist

Ensure your residential construction plans, additions, or structural modifications meet Douglas County building division compliance guidelines.

1. Site Plan Specifications

All building permit applications in Douglas County require a detailed, scaled site plan. The site plan must be prepared on a minimum sheet size of 8.5" x 11" and must explicitly showcase property dimensions, street locations, easements, setbacks, and existing structures.

If your property relies on an individual well or septic system, their exact locations must be marked on the site plan. You must also display existing and proposed topography contours, indicating slopes and drainage patterns to ensure water run-off does not affect adjacent properties.

2. Structural Engineering Requirements

Given Colorado's unique environmental conditions, residential structures must be designed to withstand local wind and snow loads. Douglas County requires sealed structural drawings prepared by a licensed Colorado Professional Engineer (P.E.).

  • Design Loads: Foundations and roofs must support a minimum roof snow load (typically 30-40 psf depending on elevation) and meet local wind speed ratings (minimum 115 mph wind load design).
  • Soils Report: A site-specific geological soils report is required to verify the foundation design can withstand expansive swelling clay soils common in Douglas County.

3. Erosion and Sedimentation Control

Prior to breaking ground, builders must establish erosion controls to comply with state clean water regulations. For projects disturbing any land, you must submit an **Erosion and Sediment Control Plan (CBMP)** showing how you will prevent soil runoff during construction.

This includes placement of silt fencing, stabilized construction entry/exit tracks, and stockpiling strategies. Inspections of these controls must be logged regularly.

4. Electronic Document Review and Inspections

Douglas County processes building permits online through the citizen portal. Plans must be uploaded as multi-page PDF documents. The plan review process takes approximately 10-15 business days for residential plans.

Once approved and construction begins, you must schedule inspections at critical phases: foundation/footings, framing, electrical rough-ins, plumbing rough-ins, insulation, and final inspection for the Certificate of Occupancy (CO).

Frequently Asked Questions

What building codes are currently active in Douglas County?

Douglas County enforces the 2018 International Residential Code (IRC), International Building Code (IBC), and local amendments.

How long is a residential building permit valid?

Permits expire if work does not commence within 180 days of issue, or if construction is suspended/abandoned for 180 days.
